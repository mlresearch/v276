title: 'DCVLR: Data Curation for Vision Language Reasoning Competition'
abstract: At NeurIPS 2025, we presented the first data-centric, open-data and open-models
  competition for data curation in vision-language reasoning. The competition focused
  on improving visual reasoning capabilities of vision-language models (VLMs) through
  data curation under a fixed supervised-finetuning (SFT) configuration and budget.
  Submissions were evaluated by fine-tuning a fixed VLM on the curated datasets and
  measuring performance on reasoning benchmarks of multiple-choice visual reasoning
  questions spanning diverse sources. The competition provided all necessary resources,
  including fine-tuning scripts, evaluation code, and baselines. Additionally, as
  a first of competitions of this kind, organizers also ran the entire SFT and evaluation
  pipeline on behalf of the contestants, thereby removing a key barrier to entry present
  in previous data-centric competitions. The result was the largest data-centric competition
  to date, with 130 signups and 20 submissions. DCVLR centered data curation as the
  key variable for scientific progress in VLM adaptation, highlighting its importance
  for real-world applications such as education, biomedicine, and scientific reasoning.
