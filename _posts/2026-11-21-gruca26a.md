title: Weather4cast at NeurIPS 2023 – Data Fusion for Quantitative Hi-Res Rain Movie
  Prediction under Spatio-temporal Shifts
abstract: Now in its third year, Weather4cast 2023 again advanced modern algorithms
  in AI and machine learning through a highly topical interdisciplinary challenge
  at the NeurIPS Competition Track. Predicting hi-res rain radar movies from multi-band
  satellite sensors required data fusion of complementary signal sources and video
  frame prediction with super-resolution techniques. This year, quantitative prediction
  of rain rates was assessed using a balanced multi-class metric. Accurate rain predictions
  are ever more critical for everyone, with climate change increasing the frequency
  of extreme precipitation events. Weather4cast models thus have a particular impact
  for the many regions on Earth where costly weather radar data are not available.
  We here present highlights and insights emerging from the competition. To reward
  models extracting relevant mechanistic patterns reflecting the underlying complex
  weather systems, our evaluation tested their responses to spatio-temporal shifts.
  Algorithms needed to forecast 4 h or 8 h of ground-based hi-res precipitation radar
  from lo-res satellite spectral images in a unique cross-sensor prediction challenge,
  and were evaluated within and across a variety of regions on Earth with diverse
  climate and different distributions of heavy precipitation events. Robustness to
  distributional shifts over time was achieved by testing predictions on data one
  year after the training period. Notably, the capability of models to correctly capture
  spatio-temporal patterns qualitatively varied considerably and was not always in
  line with leaderboard scores. This suggests that more sophisticated metrics will
  be required to accurately reflect the effectiveness of AI models in corresponding
  meteorological applications. Weather4cast will continue to explore the expansive
  data set and advance benchmark challenges in the field en route to validated foundation
  models for weather.
