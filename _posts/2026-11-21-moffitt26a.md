title: The 2025 Google Code Golf Championship
abstract: For over half a decade, the Abstraction and Reasoning Corpus has played
  a critical role in evaluating the fluid intelligence and skill acquisition efficiency
  of machine learning systems. While the dataset has proven especially useful in advancing
  program synthesis, few investments have been made in distilling the transformational
  logic of these tasks into their most irreducible computational forms. In conjunction
  with the NeurIPS 2025 Competition Track, we organized the 2025 Google Code Golf
  Championship, a three-month online challenge designed to elicit minimal Python solutions
  for all training puzzles in the seminal ARC-AGI-1 benchmark suite. Sponsored by
  Google DeepMind and funded with $100,000 in prizes, the contest attracted over 1,000
  teams and yielded tens of thousands of verified, open-source programs that collectively
  establish tight upper bounds on the Kolmogorov complexity of each task. In this
  paper, we offer a comprehensive retrospective of the event, including an overview
  of the contest format, a survey of approaches taken by top teams, and a discussion
  of potential implications for the future design of intelligent agents.
