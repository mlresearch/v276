title: Lessons Learned from Auto-Bidding in the Large-Scale Auctions Competition at
  NeurIPS 2024
software: https://github.com/alimama-tech/AuctionNet
abstract: Decision-making in large-scale games is an essential research area in artificial
  intelligence, with significant real-world impact. An agent faces the critical task
  of making high-frequency strategic decisions in an uncertain and competitive environment.
  This environment is characterized by substantial randomness and rapidly changing
  strategies from numerous competitors. However, the shortage of large-scale, realistic
  game systems and datasets has hindered research progress in this area. To provide
  opportunities for in-depth research on this valuable problem, we have presented
  the Auto-Bidding in Large-Scale Auctions Competition, derived from online advertising,
  at NeurIPS 2024. This competition focuses on bidding decision-making in uncertain
  and competitive games. It specifically includes the AIGB (AI-Generated Bidding)
  track, which emphasizes the innovation of generative algorithms in the field of
  auto-bidding. This competition has attracted over 1,500 teams from around the world.
  After six months of intense competition, participants have proposed numerous innovative
  and forward-looking solutions, significantly promoting research and technological
  development in this field. In this competition, we have open-sourced AuctionNet,
  a benchmark for bidding decision-making in large-scale ad auctions derived from
  a real-world online advertising platform. We have also pre-generated a substantial
  dataset comprising 10 million ad opportunities, 48 diverse auto-bidding agents,
  and over 500 million auction records. Performance evaluations of baseline algorithms,
  such as linear programming, reinforcement learning, and generative models, are also
  presented as part of AuctionNet. AuctionNet has powered the competition at NeurIPS
  2024. We believe that the proposed competition and AuctionNet provide opportunities
  for more researchers to gain insights and conduct research in this field, driving
  technical innovation for both academic research and real-world practical applications.
